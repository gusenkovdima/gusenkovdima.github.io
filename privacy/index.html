Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy applies to the personal apps and automations listed on this site. They are operated for private or family use and are not offered as a public commercial service.

Data used by the apps

An app may process information explicitly provided by its owner, operational metadata needed to run the app, and data made available through services the owner chooses to connect, such as Google Drive.

Google user data

When an app connects to Google services, Google user data is accessed only for the functionality described on that app's page and only after the owner grants OAuth consent.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred to unrelated third parties. Access is limited to the owner-controlled app and infrastructure providers required to operate it.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Application data and OAuth credentials are stored only in owner-controlled systems or in the connected service itself. OAuth credentials and tokens are not intentionally published in source repositories or public pages.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for the operational needs of the relevant personal app. Because these apps are operated for the owner's own use, the owner controls deletion from both the app's storage and connected services such as Google Drive.

App-specific details

Individual app pages may describe more specific data use. If an app-specific description conflicts with this general policy, the more specific description applies to that app.
